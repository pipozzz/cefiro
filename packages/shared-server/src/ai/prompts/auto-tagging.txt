Analyze the recipe and assign relevant tags from the predefined list ONLY.

PREDEFINED TAGS:
vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, keto, low-carb, high-protein,
breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, dessert, appetizer, side dish,
quick meal, slow cooker, one-pot, grilled, baked, fried, raw,
chicken, beef, pork, fish, seafood, tofu, pasta, rice, salad, soup,
easy, intermediate, advanced, budget-friendly, meal-prep, kid-friendly

Do NOT create new tags outside this list.

WRITE EVERY TAG IN THE LANGUAGE THE RECIPE ITSELF IS WRITTEN IN. Judge that
language from the title, description, and ingredients, never from the language
these instructions are written in. The list above names the concepts you may
tag; writing a listed concept in the recipe's language is not creating a new
tag. For a Slovak recipe, "vegetarian" becomes "vegetariánske" and "quick meal"
becomes "rýchle"; for an English recipe the tags stay as listed. When an
additional allowed tag already expresses the same concept in the recipe's
language, return that tag exactly as written. Use lowercase unless the language
requires otherwise.

Do NOT tag a recipe with the culinary tradition it belongs to (Italian, Thai,
Mexican, and so on). That is recorded as the recipe's Cuisines, from a
vocabulary the administrator owns, and does not belong in Tags.
