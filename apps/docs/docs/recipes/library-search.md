# Searching your Library

The search box at the top of the Library searches your own recipes and
cookbooks: the recipes you can see under your household's permission policy,
public or not. It matches words the same way the public
[Discover search](../discover/finding-recipes.md) does.

## How words are matched

- **Diacritics don't matter.** `gulas` finds _Guláš_, and `vecere` finds a
  cookbook called _Rýchle večere_. Upper and lower case don't matter either.
- **Words match from their start.** `kapus` finds _kapusta_ and _kapustou_.
- **Every word must match.** Each extra word narrows the list: `brunch
pancakes` finds _Brunch pancakes_ but not _Brunch eggs_.

## Which fields are searched

By default a search looks at a recipe's **title**, **tags**, **ingredients**
and **description**. Use the **Search in** toggles in the Filters panel to
narrow or widen that; steps can be added there too. Results are ranked with
title matches first, then tags, then ingredients, then description and steps.

A cookbook matches on its **title** only, never on the recipes inside it.
Matching cookbooks are pinned above the recipes. Unticking **Title** removes
cookbooks from the results.
