# Publishing & sharing recipes

Every recipe has a **visibility**:

- **Private** — only you (and your household) can see it. The default.
- **Link only** — anyone with the link can open it, but it is not listed in
  Discover, search or the sitemap.
- **Public** — listed in Discover and search, on your public profile, and in the
  sitemap for search engines.

You can set visibility on a single recipe from its page, or on many at once in
the sharing manager.

## Sharing many at once

**Library → Manage sharing** (`/library/sharing`) lists all your recipes. Tick
the ones you want, then choose **Make public**, **Link only** or **Make
private**. It is the fast way to turn an existing library into public recipes
that Discover and search can reach.

## Copyright: imported recipes

A recipe you **imported** from another website may carry that site's photos and
wording — content you may not have the right to republish. Cefiro guards against
publishing it by accident:

- **Single recipe** — publishing an imported recipe shows a warning first. If you
  genuinely hold the rights, you can go ahead.
- **In bulk** — the sharing manager **will not** make imported recipes public. A
  "Make public" sweep skips them and tells you how many were skipped, because a
  bulk action gives no chance to judge each one. Imported recipes are marked with
  an **Imported** badge so you can see which is which. Link-only and private are
  unaffected.

Recipes you created in Cefiro yourself — including ones you saved from other
cooks on the instance — are not restricted; they publish normally.

## Unpublish imported

If imported recipes were made public before these guardrails existed, **Manage
sharing** shows an **Unpublish imported** button when any of your imported
recipes are still public or link-only. One click sets them all back to private
and removes them from Discover and search. It only touches your own recipes, and
never your authored ones.
